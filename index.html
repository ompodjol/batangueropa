About Batang Europa

Batang Europa's mission is to showcase the best top prospects from Europe, specifically those with Filipino heritage, and provide them a platform to compete at the highest levels and the opportunity to be recruited by schools in the Philippines. Based in Stockholm, Sweden, our program blends skill development with discipline, teamwork, and sportsmanship, preparing players for competition at every level.

Our Programs

Coach Kem — Head Coach, U19

Kem Navoa leads Batang Europa's U19 squad, guiding selected players through the Global Hoops International Showcase and NBTC national selection process in Manila. A believer in tough love and high standards, Coach Kem is known for pushing players — especially guards — to be "extra exceptional."
